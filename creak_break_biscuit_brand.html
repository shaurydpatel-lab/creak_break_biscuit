Our Story

How The Creak & Break Legacy Began

Founded with a passion for authentic baking, Creak & Break started in a small family kitchen with a simple mission: to create a biscuit that honors traditional wholesomeness while delivering an unbeatable sensory experience.

We noticed that mass-produced biscuits relied heavily on refined flours and artificial preservatives. We chose a different path—dedicating ourselves entirely to 100% pure wheat flour, natural flavor infusions, and an artisanal baking process that gives our biscuits their signature satisfying crunch.
